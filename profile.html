The longer version

I finished high school in Mumbai, studied Computer Science and Philosophy at the University of Illinois, and I'm now doing a master's in Computer Science & Engineering at the University of Michigan.

I care about how technology shapes who gets to participate: in computing, in communities, in the future. That's why I like the parts of computing where the philosophical and the technical are impossible to separate.

Away from a keyboard, I really enjoy painting and writing. I also enjoy dancing and singing, though some argue I should stay away from the latter.

I'm also a devoted older sister, which I have written about.

About this site: the purple and blue paint is thistle and powder blue, the same colors as the first version I wrote by hand in 2021. The dot at the top of the page switches to other colors. Go ahead.
